Electrification works best as a whole-home project.

Electrification means replacing—or partially replacing—equipment that burns natural gas, oil, or propane with efficient electric technology. It is not simply an equipment swap.

The best plan considers heat loss, insulation, air leakage, ductwork, ventilation, electrical capacity, equipment sizing, and the way each system affects the rest of your home. EcoPower begins with the data, then builds a prioritized path around your needs.

Common questions

Electrification, explained.

Do I need to electrify my entire home at once?

No. A plan may be full, partial, or phased. Many homeowners begin with an audit, improve the envelope, and time equipment changes around condition, budget, comfort needs, and incentive eligibility.

Why start with an energy audit?

An audit reveals air leakage, insulation gaps, duct conditions, safety concerns, and the home’s energy profile. That information helps create a better scope and supports BGE-approved energy modeling when pursuing Home Performance rebates.

Will a heat pump keep my home comfortable in winter?

Modern heat pumps are designed for year-round heating and cooling. The right answer depends on equipment selection, design temperature, sizing, the building envelope, ductwork, and whether a full-electric or hybrid approach best fits the home.

Are incentives guaranteed?

No. Eligibility and amounts depend on the customer account, property, selected measures, modeled savings, project cost, installation requirements, available funding, and current program rules. EcoPower helps verify the applicable pathway before work proceeds.
